# SingMotion AI — Search discovery and keyword strategy (2026-11-16)

## Aim and launch constraint

Make SingMotion discoverable for **what the application actually does**: transform
a permitted portrait and the user's own audio into a singing/musical AI
performance video with a directed scene, account-owned project and private MP4.

The app's global public Checkout has **not yet been approved for launch**. Until
the existing public-credit release gate is genuinely ready, Production remains
password-gated and crawler **noindex**; Staging remains blocked regardless of
SEO settings. Never make a site public, remove the gate or issue a charge for
the purpose of SEO. Once public release is independently approved, crawler
metadata, sitemap and targeted pages become indexable automatically.

This is a **priority/intention map**, NOT measured keyword volume, current
ranking, verified Search Console data or a prediction of organic traffic.
Real keyword prioritization must be adjusted using query impressions, ranking,
CTR and sign-up / paid-conversion data after Search Console is available.

## Feature and brand claim discipline

- **Yes:** musician-supplied music/audio, an authorized image, AI animation
  via the reviewed BytePlus OmniHuman 2.0 integration, scene/camera direction,
  landscape/portrait selection, owner-private MP4 and credits when enabled.
- **Never promise:** automatic composition of music, an unrestricted
  text-to-video model, voice cloning, guaranteed pixel-perfect lip sync,
  automatic TikTok/Reels/YouTube upload, free/unlimited generations,
  commercial rights in third-party media, any guaranteed audience reach,
  or universal worldwide payment availability.
- OmniHuman / BytePlus references must clearly identify the external
  technology and state that SingMotion is an **independent** application.
  Never title a page "Official OmniHuman" or imply affiliation.
- Do not add schema.org ratings, review counts, "free" offers, customer
  testimonials or prices that have not been independently substantiated.
- Social platform and music terms are useful search intents, not an
  endorsement or integration claim.

## Implemented technical SEO

- English and German **server-rendered** hub and 4 topic pages each
  (10 localized documents total), plus original homepage.
- Unique descriptive page titles/descriptions, canonical URLs,
  \`hreflang\` \`de\` / \`en\` / \`x-default\`,
  Open Graph/Twitter descriptions and an authentic product image.
- Correct H1/H2 structure; concrete how-to steps, limitations, FAQs and
  internal links from the homepage, navigation and related-topic sections.
- \`WebApplication\` and \`BreadcrumbList\` JSON-LD that describes reality;
  no fabricated social proof, prices or fake FAQ rich-result claims.
- Runtime release-gated \`/robots.txt\` and \`/sitemap.xml\`; no indexable
  sitemap before public sign-off. Staging and private Production keep
  \`X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow\`; public user-specific HTML routes remain
  noindex as well.
- Dynamic \`/studio\`, \`/credits\`, account, sign-in, private projects and
  payment endpoints are not included in the public sitemap.

1. After public launch approval, anonymously fetch:
   \`https://www.singmotion.ai/robots.txt\`,
   \`https://www.singmotion.ai/sitemap.xml\`, homepage and the ten localized
   landing pages; verify 200/robots/canonicals/hreflang from a separate,
   non-authenticated browser. Before public release these *must* remain
   inaccessible or noindexed.
2. Verify the **URL-prefix or domain property** for singmotion.ai in
   Google Search Console and (if useful) Bing Webmaster Tools using an
   operator-controlled DNS / site ownership method, without publishing
   account credentials. Submit the sitemap only when URLs actually serve
   unauthenticated public HTML.
3. Check Google's URL Inspection and Rich Results Test for rendered
   content, canonical selection, JSON-LD and indexing status. Fix discovered
   rendering or mobile usability errors before scaling content.
4. Establish baseline for **impressions, clicks, CTR and average position**
   separately for DE/EN by URL and query. Segment brand and non-brand searches.
   This requires connected/verified Search Console data; no claims of actual
   rankings or volumes until measured.
5. Add conversion events with lawful privacy/consent controls:
   \`landing_page_view\` where permissible, \`studio_started\`,
   \`signup_completed\`, \`credit_checkout_started\`,
   \`credit_checkout_paid\` and \`video_downloaded\`. Never send names, media,
   raw checkout IDs or private prompts to advertising analytics. Events are a
   future proposal; **tracking is not deployed by this SEO release**.
6. Review queries monthly. Expand only where a real new feature,
   actual search demand and original helpful content justify a page:
   authorized artist portraits, music video storyboarding, vertical-safe
   framing, rights and workflow tutorials. Avoid vanity "rank #1" claims.
